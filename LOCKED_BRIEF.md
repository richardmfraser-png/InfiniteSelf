# Infinite Self — locked refinement brief, v3.0

## Purpose
Continue the existing local-first personal practice app: mental/physical commitments, meditation, goals, reading, reflection and journaling. This release refines that app rather than replacing the user's records.

## Identity and photography
Name: Infinite Self. Keep the blue visual direction and signature wave/light photograph. Replace the Journal image with better-framed existing imagery. Do not put captions, signature labels or title badges over photographs. Allow the user to pick another page photograph from Gallery.

## Main refinements
- Session continuity across in-app pages and an elapsed-time timer visible in a persistent player dock.
- Device-supported background playback for native/local audio; visible YouTube playback only.
- Saved YouTube links and permitted local audio import.
- Optional dictation with keyboard fallback, alongside ordinary text entry.
- Clear category → commitment → branch/task → metric hierarchy and completion visuals on Today.
- Same-page Wisdom reflection composer, type/date selection, text/dictation, append-to-Journal action.
- Seeded photo gallery with user uploads, favourites, uncropped viewing and device-local storage.

## Preserve
Elective setup; four foundational commitments with at least two mental and two physical; optional branches; discrete goals and milestones; six Wisdom categories; Help; progress; backups; the existing records key and hosted location.

## Platform limits are part of the brief
Do not promise hidden YouTube playback, universal background sound, universal dictation or a cloud-synced gallery. State these clearly in Help and implementation notes. Existing uploaded photographs are sufficient to ship this release; additional photos are optional later additions.


## v3.1 refinement addendum
SoundCloud Daimoku in addition to YouTube; source-aware playback rates; editable Guiding Intention; central Settings backup/restore/export; adjustable page/card palettes and font size; text-input following across typing/dictation. Existing local records and media must be preserved. Do not imply unsupported SoundCloud rate or background-play guarantees.
