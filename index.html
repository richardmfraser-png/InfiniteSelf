Backup, restore & export

A full backup contains your private journal, records, saved links and personal photos/audio. It is not encrypted. Keep it private. Sharing the app files does not share your entries.

Full and records-only backups include your Guiding Intention, appearance and audio-speed preferences. Linked SoundCloud/YouTube recordings are not downloaded. Activity CSV excludes journal text; readable journal exports include it. Keep all personal exports out of GitHub.

This is a browser estimate, not a device-wide measurement. Exporting creates a download; it does not prove the file was saved or synced.
