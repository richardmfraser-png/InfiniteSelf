Your audio library

Save a title and a video link. The actual YouTube player stays visible while you move between app pages. An internet connection and permission to embed the video are needed.

Own-file playback is the best option for switching apps. Use recordings you own or have permission to use. MP3/M4A work on many phones; test your device. Maximum 40 MB per file.

Continuity, with clear limits

Typing and moving through Infinite Self will not restart the sound or timer. YouTube pauses when the app is hidden. The included audio files and your own uploads use device media controls where supported, but background and lock-screen playback depend on the device. Dictation deliberately pauses sound to avoid recording the speaker.

Help & FAQ

Infinite Self v3.0

Start with one intentional day.

Run the optional setup or skip it. Name your journey, set four commitments, and add goals when useful. Today is for action; Journal is for noticing; Progress is for adjustment.

Back up before you update or change devices

A full backup contains your private journal, records, saved links and personal photos/audio. It is not encrypted. Keep it private. Sharing the app files does not share your entries.
