## One intentional day

Open **Today**, choose your date, set **My 3**, and log the practices you actually completed. Previous/Next, the date picker and Simulate date let you review or correct older days. Future dates are for planning priorities, not logging completed activity.

## Build a rhythm

Go to **Routines**. Start with **Morning practice** or **Evening reset**, or choose **Build a routine**. Edit the order, turn steps on/off, add a custom practice, and select your meditation duration/audio, Wisdom category and journal type.

Tap **Begin this practice**. A guide follows you through the existing screens. Complete the work there, then choose **Done & next**. **Skip** is always available; **Pause** keeps your place. Resume from Today or Routines. Your journal/session is saved once, not duplicated by the routine tracker.

## Write with an image

In **Gallery**, tap a photograph. Swipe left/right, use Previous/Next, or use keyboard arrows. **Edit details** sets a name, caption and tags inside the app; the original device file is unchanged. **Save a copy** suggests the chosen title as the filename.

Choose **Reflect on this image**, select the date and Morning/Freeform/Evening, and type or dictate. Saving appends the response and its source link to Journal. The checkbox also adds a photo of the day. To attach an image without writing, choose **Photo of the day** in the viewer.

## Review the week

Choose **Progress → Weekly review** and select any day in the week. Review My 3, practices, saved meditation sessions, journaling, readings, milestones and routine activity. Weeks run Monday–Sunday; an unfinished week is labelled accordingly.

Answer **What helped? What got in the way? What will I adjust?** Add one focus, save the review, and optionally choose **Use as next week’s focus**. Past reviews remain editable and exportable as text.

## Keep the foundation

**Commitments**: at least two mental and two physical commitments. Add branches where useful. Targets are configurable. Weekly progress counts qualifying days rather than treating twice the minutes on one day as two practice days.

**Goals**: outcomes, target dates, linked practices and milestones. **Journal**: morning, freeform, evening, tags, date selection, autosave, full searchable entries and source links. **Wisdom**: one unread piece at a time, plus read history and hearts. **Meditation**: native audio, permitted uploads, SoundCloud/YouTube links, the timer and the one-tap gong.

Typing and switching app tabs do not rebuild the meditation player. Dictation deliberately pauses sound. YouTube pauses when its video is hidden, including a tucked dock; restore the video to play again. SoundCloud and native audio keep their in-app dock behaviour, subject to the provider/browser. Device background playback is not guaranteed.

## Protect your material

**Settings → Backup & export** shows the last full-backup download initiated, changes since it, and the last file you explicitly confirmed saving. Export a full backup regularly and after important writing or photo uploads. A download starting does not prove a saved copy exists.

Use **Recently removed** in that section to restore a removed upload or activity entry. Photos there still occupy storage until **Delete permanently** is chosen. This is not a cloud recycle bin and does not survive clearing browser data without a backup.
